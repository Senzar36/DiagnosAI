Welcome to KinCare

This app allows users to maintain a daily record of their health updates.

Users can log their health status, upload their medical tests and reports.

This will allow doctors to better understand the patient's condition and provide more effective treatment.

This application allows doctors to better keep track of the patient's health information. This helps in making informed decisions about the patient's care.

This will also improve communication between patients and healthcare providers. It will reduce the risk of patient forgetting important details that might have happened.

The in-built AI assistant will summarize all of the patient's health information, making it easier for doctors to review and analyze. It will also highlight any concerning trends or patterns.

Note : The AI assistant will not provide any medical device. It is built solely for the purpose of summarizing health information.
